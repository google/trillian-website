<div class="font-google font-medium">

Following up on the Verifiable Index (VIndex) prototype
presented last year, this talk presents an updated architecture designed to
support verifiable search at Certificate Transparency (CT) and Merkle Tree
Certificate (MTC) scale. While the code remains an evolving, experimental
implementation, the design has matured to address hard production
realities: high-fanout storage layouts, deterministic sandboxed execution,
and crash-resilient pipeline lifecycles. Backed by benchmark data from
indexing hundreds of millions of public log entries, we will explore what
has changed since the initial prototype and the practical engineering
lessons learned along the way.

---

### Speaker

Martin Hutchinson is a software engineer on the TrustFabric team at
Google, where he works on transparency and verifiable systems. He is the
core author of the Claimant Model, and a maintainer of several
specifications at C2SP, including tlog-tiles. As a core maintainer of the
TrustFabric open source libraries, he has worked on projects such as
Trillian, Tessera, and the Armored Witness.
