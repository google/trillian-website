Merkle Tree Certificates (MTCs) are more than a serialization or transport
optimization since they change the way trust is built. This is a challenge for
European qualified trust service providers (QTSPs) and the regulatory
supervision. The talk will sketch challenges and advantages when applying MTC
concepts to the eIDAS ecosystem, like Cosigner role, certificate revocation,
interplay with Qualified Website Authentication Certificates (QWACs), Qualified
Signatures and other eIDAS services. MTCs are not presented as a ready-made
replacement for today’s infrastructure, but to identify the protocol,
governance and policy questions that must be resolved before Merkle-based
certificates can support European trust services at scale.

### Speaker

Jan Klaußner is Senior Product Architect at Bundesdruckerei in Berlin with more
than two decades of experience in security engineering focusing on innovative
solutions for digital identity and trust services. In his current work, he
develops and evaluates prototypes for quantum‑safe public key infrastructures
and hybrid post‑quantum cryptography deployments in real‑world environments. Jan
is actively involved in standardization efforts in IETF, ISO and ETSI on hybrid
PQC schemes for Internet PKI and smart‑card ecosystems. He regularly shares
insights at international conferences and industry events.
